- My calendar says 'free at four,' which proves it has never met my to-do list.
- The group chat called itself 'Urgent' and immediately used the name for cat photos.
- I opened the fridge for a sign; it showed me the leftovers from 2019.
- I told my mirror to be honest, and it started with the hair, because of course it did.
- I cleaned my room and found the floor, which had been taking requests.
- I put 'drink water' on my to-do list, checked it off, then drank coffee to celebrate.
